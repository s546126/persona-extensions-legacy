# Termwise

A reading page with two modes: translation keeps the article and adds a translation under each block, and vocabulary underlines candidate words so they can be opened and remembered.

- [Guide](../../docs/extensions/termwise.md)
- [Manifest](persona-extension.json)
- [Companion CLI (`s546126/termwise`)](https://github.com/s546126/termwise)
- [Development and tests](../../docs/development.md)

Word glosses are a subset of ECDICT, and candidate filtering aligns with [s546126/termwise](https://github.com/s546126/termwise). See [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
